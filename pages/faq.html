Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about HIS System

What is HIS System?

HIS (Healthcare Information System) is a comprehensive platform for managing healthcare operations including patient management, clinical data, appointments, laboratory tests, and more.

What are the system requirements?

You need Node.js 16+, PHP 8.0+, MySQL 8.0+, and Git installed on your system.

Is HIS System open source?

Yes, HIS System is 100% open source and available under the MIT License.

How do I get support?

You can reach our support team at support@his-system.local or open an issue on GitHub.

Can I customize HIS System?

Yes, HIS System is fully customizable. You can modify the source code to fit your specific needs.

What databases are supported?

Currently, HIS System supports MySQL 8.0+. Support for other databases can be added.
